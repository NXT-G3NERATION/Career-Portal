# 🚀 Career Portal

Welcome to **Career Portal**—a community-driven, open-source hub designed to centralize top-tier career pathways, company career pages, and internship portals. 

Whether you are a student hunting for your next big opportunity or a developer looking to build your open-source resume, **you are in the right place.** 

---

## 🌟 Why Contribute?
Contributing to this repository isn't just about sharing links; it’s about **gaining real-world experience**. Every merged pull request (PR) counts as an official open-source contribution that you can feature on your GitHub profile, resume, and LinkedIn.

* **Build your portfolio:** Show future employers that you actively collaborate in public codebases.
* **Master version control:** Practice professional developer workflows (Forks, Branches, Pull Requests).
* **Help the community:** Make the job search easier for thousands of students and early-career professionals.
